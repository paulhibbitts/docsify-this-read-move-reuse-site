# Why It Matters

<small>Part 1 of 6</small>

> [!TIP]
> **Learning Objectives**
>
> - **Read:** see how both starters are designed for readers and learners
> - **Move:** take content between tools, and bring it in from Pressbooks or an LMS
> - **Reuse:** get any page's Markdown, for your own purposes

Hello there, I'm Paul Hibbitts – after 25 years teaching user interface design, mostly at SFU, I now build free, open-source tools that turn Markdown into websites for open educators and publishers.

**Open both ways:** content that's open for readers often stays inside the platform for its authors – harder to edit elsewhere, move or keep. What if it were open for both?

This guide shows two free tools that are designed for readers and learners, let authors move their content, and let anyone reuse it.

> [!NOTE]
> **The DIY answer: plain Markdown**
>
> A simple text format for web pages – `**bold**`, `## Heading`, `- list item`. It's supported almost everywhere, from GitHub and note-taking apps to Google Docs, and it's widely used by other tools, including AI. Keep every page in Markdown, on a free site you control.

**Reuse anywhere:** anyone can get the Markdown behind any page they're viewing, with its callouts, math and diagrams intact – so it opens as it is in [Docsify-This](https://docsify-this.net/) or on GitHub.

No slides needed: the original version of this guide works as a presentation (← and → move between pages), a web page to read at your own pace, an LMS embed, a printout or Markdown – all from a single source. Write once, use anywhere.

**See it moved:** this same guide also runs, with the same content, on [the Quark 2 theme](https://read-move-reuse.hibbittsdesign.org/guide) and [the classic Quark theme](https://one.on-rev.com/grav/read-move-reuse-ops-quark/guide), as an [Open MultiCourse Hub course](https://one.on-rev.com/grav/read-move-reuse-mch/guide), and on Grav Premium Helios as an [open reader](https://one.on-rev.com/grav/read-move-reuse-hor/guide) and a [course hub](https://one.on-rev.com/grav/read-move-reuse-hch/guide).
